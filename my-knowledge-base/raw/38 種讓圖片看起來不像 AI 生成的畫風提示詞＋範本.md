## 38 種讓圖片看起來不像 AI 生成的畫風提示詞＋範本

## AI圖片生成的「AI感」消除用畫風提示詞集（38種風格＋範本）

用 ChatGPT（GPT Image）做資料或網站用插圖時，常常會卡在同一個地方。

線條太過一致、漸層太平滑、臉部太過工整、背景裡還會莫名多出一些毫無意義的小物件。簡單來說，有時候一眼就看得出來是 AI 生成的圖片。

所以我做了 38 種只要接在日文指示句後面就能固定畫風的提示詞，並且全部用同一個題目實際生成後排在一起比較。哪些有效事前並不知道，因此我是實際逐一測試的。也一併整理了用來「降低 AI 感」的附加區塊，以及可以直接拿來用的完成版範本。

內容以複製貼上為前提設計，所以你只要把有興趣的畫風程式碼區塊拿去用就夠了。

## 基本的使用方式

做法只有兩個步驟。

1. 先用日文寫出想畫的內容
2. 接在後面貼上這篇文章中選擇的提示詞

為了統一，我沒有加太細的指示，而是反覆使用 **「請畫一位日本少女」** 。

接著在這後面加入 STYLE 提示詞。以下所有生成範例都是用同一題目做的，方便你只比較畫風差異。

把 STYLE 提示詞寫成英文，是因為表現畫風的詞彙（linework、halftone、registration 等）通常用英文比較容易有效。內容用日文、畫風用英文，這個組合目前最穩定。

## 扁平・編輯風格系

### 10\. 簡潔扁平設計

```
STYLE:
clean flat illustration, simple geometric shapes, limited color palette, minimal shading, clear silhouettes, balanced negative space, restrained visual details, subtle asymmetry, friendly editorial design, simple vector-like forms without excessive polish
```

[![style-10-flat-simple.png](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2Ffc525740-37c4-4937-9816-346489b9123b.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=7d95e0742e1aaed3cfdbeafbb3312319)](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2Ffc525740-37c4-4937-9816-346489b9123b.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=7d95e0742e1aaed3cfdbeafbb3312319)

就是一般常見的扁平插畫。不過只靠這個指定很容易變成光滑得很像 AI 的圖，所以也建議一起試試下一個 11。

### 11\. AI感較弱的扁平設計

`flat illustration` 單獨使用時很容易變成 AI 特有的光滑向量插畫，所以我故意加入了一些「刻意不完美」的設定。

```
STYLE:
human-designed flat illustration, simple geometric forms with subtle irregularities, slightly imperfect curves, restrained color palette, minimal shading, intentional asymmetry, varied proportions, natural spacing, subtle print-like texture, editorial graphic design, avoid overly polished vector aesthetics
```

[![style-11-flat-human-made.png](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2F827899ca-07a0-4a4e-addf-3f35eecaab4b.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=ef66fa735a1efa7ceb9b958453df02f5)](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2F827899ca-07a0-4a4e-addf-3f35eecaab4b.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=ef66fa735a1efa7ceb9b958453df02f5)

`intentional asymmetry` （刻意的不對稱）和 `subtle print-like texture` （些微印刷質感）這兩個設定很有效。跟 10 放在一起看，差異會非常明顯。

### 18\. 塗鴉・塗鴉風


會有較高的頭身比例，並且特徵是只在部分區域上色。

## 用來削弱「AI感」的附加區塊

這些是加在前面各種 STYLE 後面使用的附加內容。因為有分成不同目的，所以只要貼上會對應症狀的那一段就夠了。

### 保留手作感

加在你喜歡的 STYLE 最後面。這一組很泛用，基本上第一個先記住這個就可以。

```
HUMAN-MADE CHARACTER:
preserve natural imperfections, subtle asymmetry, irregular line pressure, varied spacing, slightly imperfect curves, restrained detail, natural shape variation, handmade texture, understated visual decisions, avoid excessive visual perfection
```

### 避免滑順的 AI 插畫

把太過平滑的向量曲線、光澤、像塑膠一樣的表面，直接點名禁止。

```
Avoid overly smooth vector curves, glossy gradients, excessive rim lighting, plastic-looking surfaces, hyper-clean outlines, perfectly symmetrical faces, excessive tiny details, generic stock-illustration aesthetics, overly polished digital rendering.
```

### 避免資訊塞太滿

AI 圖如果不加限制，畫面裡的東西會一直增加。明確要求留白其實很有效。

```
Keep the composition simple and intentional. Use only necessary objects. Leave generous negative space. Avoid decorative clutter, unnecessary background objects, excessive accessories, random visual symbols, and over-detailed environments.
```

### 讓人物更自然

禁止不自然的笑容和像模特兒一樣的姿勢。

```
Characters should have natural relaxed poses, subtle asymmetry, believable body language, simple expressive gestures, understated facial expressions, and slightly varied proportions. Avoid mannequin-like posing and exaggerated artificial smiles.
```

### 讓兒童插畫更自然

畫小孩時常常會讓比例和表情過頭，所以要另外加上兒童專用設定。

```
Children should have age-appropriate proportions, simple natural expressions, casual body language, slightly imperfect clothing folds, ordinary hairstyles, and believable everyday poses. Keep facial details simple.
```

### 讓背景不要很 AI

可以避免同樣的物件不自然地重複，或是乍看合理、仔細看卻不成立的建築。

```
Use a simple believable background with only a few meaningful objects. Avoid repetitive objects, impossible architecture, decorative clutter, excessive depth, and random background details.
```

### 讓顏色不要很 AI

用來抑制飽和度過高、整體變成彩虹色的情況。

```
Use a restrained limited color palette, slightly muted colors, natural color relationships, subtle variations within flat areas, and avoid overly saturated rainbow-like color combinations.
```

## 可直接使用的完成版範本

接下來是把上面的 STYLE 與附加區塊組合成一條完整提示詞的版本。如果用途已經確定，直接複製這些會比較快。

### 通用範本

不知道要選哪個時就用這個。存起來後，幾乎各種場景都能用。

```
[在這裡寫你希望用日文描述的內容]

VISUAL STYLE:
hand-drawn editorial illustration, organic irregular linework, subtle variations in line thickness, simplified shapes, slightly imperfect proportions, restrained details, soft matte colors, limited harmonious color palette, subtle paper and print texture, intentional asymmetry, natural spacing, generous negative space.

HUMAN-MADE CHARACTER:
preserve small drawing imperfections, uneven line pressure, slightly irregular curves, subtle shape variations, natural visual rhythm, understated design decisions, handmade texture.

COMPOSITION:
simple clear composition, one obvious visual focus, only necessary objects, believable spatial relationships, uncluttered background.

AVOID:
overly smooth vector curves, glossy gradients, plastic-looking surfaces, excessive rim lighting, hyper-detailed rendering, perfectly symmetrical faces, generic stock illustration aesthetics, excessive decorative elements, unnecessary background objects, artificial posing, overly polished digital appearance.
```

[![template-01-universal.png](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2Fe564bbc4-6ed3-40a2-9f62-4834b01c18b1.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=2a6152c10df87472da7787f5e3b747f3)](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2Fe564bbc4-6ed3-40a2-9f62-4834b01c18b1.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=2a6152c10df87472da7787f5e3b747f3)

我把它設成 `VISUAL STYLE` / `HUMAN-MADE CHARACTER` / `COMPOSITION` / `AVOID` 這四個區塊。替換內容時，如果以區塊為單位替換，比較不容易跑掉。

### 手繪繪本向

```
[在這裡寫你希望用日文描述的內容]

Create the image as a warm hand-drawn children's book illustration.

Use loose organic outlines with subtle variations in thickness and pressure. Shapes should feel slightly irregular and naturally hand-drawn rather than geometrically perfect. Use simplified expressive characters, soft matte colors, a restrained harmonious palette, subtle paper grain, and occasional visible drawing or coloring texture.

Keep the composition calm and uncluttered, with generous empty space and only a few meaningful background elements. Facial expressions should be simple and natural.

Preserve subtle asymmetry, irregular curves, slight differences between repeated objects, and human drawing imperfections.

Avoid glossy digital rendering, smooth vector-like curves, strong gradients, excessive lighting effects, excessive details, perfectly symmetrical faces, generic stock illustration aesthetics, plastic-looking surfaces, and overly polished AI-generated appearance.
```

[![template-02-picture-book.png](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2F435aacc6-1011-4358-b99c-2f8e2cd5661a.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=9f95b1722f73008c0ee8c4f6f9057677)](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2F435aacc6-1011-4358-b99c-2f8e2cd5661a.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=9f95b1722f73008c0ee8c4f6f9057677)

這裡不是用條列式，而是用散文寫法。因為有些情況下，長文提示反而更容易有效，所以兩種寫法都保留了。

### 扁平設計向

```
[在這裡寫你希望用日文描述的內容]

Create a human-designed editorial flat illustration.

Use simple geometric shapes with subtle organic irregularities, slightly imperfect curves, simplified human figures, a restrained four-to-six-color palette, minimal shading, clear silhouettes, intentional asymmetry, and generous negative space.

Keep the forms simple and graphic. Introduce subtle variations in proportions and spacing so repeated elements do not appear mechanically identical. Add a very subtle print-like texture.

Avoid glossy gradients, 3D effects, excessive shadows, perfectly smooth vector geometry, excessive decorative elements, generic corporate stock illustration aesthetics, and overly polished AI-generated appearance.
```

[![template-03-flat.png](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2F1aed1c5f-7556-40ed-9def-5beeacd9733b.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=4105e4fccd6f22c23f46e6f4642f3d18)](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2F1aed1c5f-7556-40ed-9def-5beeacd9733b.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=4105e4fccd6f22c23f46e6f4642f3d18)

重點是加入「讓重複元素看起來不要機械式完全一致」這句。扁平風格的 AI 感，通常就是從這裡冒出來的。

### 療癒手繪向

```
[在這裡寫你希望用日文描述的內容]

Create a loose casual hand-drawn illustration.

Use simple rounded shapes, slightly wobbly outlines, uneven line thickness, minimal facial details, relaxed proportions, flat muted colors, and plenty of empty space. The drawing should feel spontaneous and charmingly imperfect, similar to an illustrator quickly drawing with a pen in a sketchbook.

Allow minor asymmetry, irregular curves, inconsistent spacing, and subtle differences between similar shapes.

Avoid perfect geometry, smooth vector lines, realistic rendering, glossy surfaces, complex lighting, excessive details, and overly polished digital aesthetics.
```

[![template-04-loose.png](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2Fc36bce3b-ce3b-4a9d-894e-e53c013a8759.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=988386e79d411c137c87545af5973714)](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2Fc36bce3b-ce3b-4a9d-894e-e53c013a8759.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=988386e79d411c137c87545af5973714)

只要加一句比喻成「像是在筆記本上用筆快速畫出來的感覺」，目的就會更容易傳達。

### Risograph 印刷向

```
[在這裡寫你希望用日文描述的內容]

Create a handmade risograph-style editorial illustration.

Use bold simplified shapes, a limited two-to-four ink color palette, visible paper grain, subtle ink noise, imperfect color registration, slightly uneven ink density, irregular handmade edges, and simplified expressive characters.

Use large graphic areas and generous negative space. Keep details deliberately sparse.

Avoid smooth gradients, photographic realism, glossy surfaces, precise vector geometry, excessive fine detail, perfect color alignment, and polished digital rendering.
```

[![template-05-risograph.png](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2Fa1bfd621-db29-4b65-8215-18e8463a18b9.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=a5a47d8720aa6fcdb57d089014b3c94c)](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2Fa1bfd621-db29-4b65-8215-18e8463a18b9.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=a5a47d8720aa6fcdb57d089014b3c94c)

把墨色限制在 2～4 色這個設定非常有效。如果目標是消除 AI 感，我會建議先從這個開始試。

### 水彩繪本向

```
[在這裡寫你希望用日文描述的內容]

Create a traditional watercolor children's book illustration.

Use loose pencil or ink outlines combined with transparent watercolor washes. Allow subtle pigment bleeding, uneven painted edges, visible watercolor paper grain, slight variations in color density, and areas where the white paper remains visible.

Keep characters simplified and expressive. Use a gentle restrained palette and a quiet uncluttered composition.

Avoid digital airbrushing, perfectly smooth gradients, glossy highlights, sharp vector edges, excessive realism, hyper-detailed rendering, and overly polished AI-generated appearance.
```

[![template-06-watercolor.png](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2F80e0fb34-38f2-4665-a729-93d02040f10c.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=94df79816d8761c95bc74f4292121e0b)](https://qiita-user-contents.imgix.net/https%3A%2F%2Fqiita-image-store.s3.ap-northeast-1.amazonaws.com%2F0%2F4074489%2F80e0fb34-38f2-4665-a729-93d02040f10c.png?ixlib=rb-4.1.1&auto=format&gif-q=60&q=75&s=94df79816d8761c95bc74f4292121e0b)

## 總結

把 38 種風格排在一起之後，我發現對消除 AI 感最有效的是這 4 件事。

- **直接要求不完美** — 像 `slightly irregular` 、 `uneven line pressure` 、 `intentional asymmetry` 這類詞，用來明確表示「不要畫得太完美」。
- **借用物理質感** — 加入紙張紋理、墨色偏移、網點等。Risograph 和版畫系之所以強，就是因為這個原因。
- **用數字限制留白與色數** — 像 `limited three-to-five-color palette` 、 `generous negative space` 這樣直接限制。放著不管，物件和色彩通常都會越來越多。
- **寫出禁止項目** — 用 `AVOID` 區塊先處理掉光澤漸層、左右完全對稱的臉等問題。

反過來說，只有 `hand-drawn` 或 `illustration` 這種詞是不夠的。這兩個詞很容易只把畫面拉回 AI 心中「常見插畫」的平均值，所以如果不把「要怎麼崩壞」寫清楚，最後還是會回到那種滑順的圖。

已經整理成可以直接貼上的形式了，建議你先挑一個喜歡的畫風，接到日文指示後面試試看。
